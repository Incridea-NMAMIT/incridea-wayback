# Incridea Wayback

Incridea Wayback preserves the websites and visual worlds of Incridea across
its editions. It is a place to return to the events, people, ideas, artwork,
and atmosphere that shaped each chapter of the festival.

## Incridea

Incridea is the national-level techno-cultural fest of NMAM Institute of
Technology, Nitte. It brings together students from institutions across India
through technology, arts, culture, and student-led initiatives.

Its name reflects the values that guide the festival: Innovation, Ideation, and
Creation. From the programme to the experiences around it, Incridea is
conceived and delivered by students. That spirit of leadership, collaboration,
and imagination is at the heart of every edition.

With more than forty events across technology, arts, and culture, Incridea has
welcomed nearly 7,000 participants and visitors in a year. It has also created
a stage for artists, performers, organisers, and student communities to make
memories together.

## NMAM Institute of Technology

NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte, was established in 1986 and is
part of Nitte (Deemed to be University), Mangaluru. The institute brings
together engineering, postgraduate, and research communities, with a culture
of learning that extends beyond the classroom.

Incridea grows from that environment. It reflects the energy of a campus where
students build, perform, organise, experiment, and turn ambitious ideas into
shared experiences.

## Why Wayback

A festival exists in a particular moment: its theme, its design language, its
events, and the people who make it happen belong to that year. When the next
edition begins, those moments deserve to remain discoverable rather than fade
away.

Wayback is Incridea's record of those moments. Each archived edition is kept as
a read-only experience, so visitors can revisit the character of a festival
year without confusing it with the current edition. It honours the work of the
students and teams behind every chapter, while carrying Incridea's story
forward.

The archive is not only a collection of old pages. It is a continuing memory
of a student-built festival and the community that gives it life.
